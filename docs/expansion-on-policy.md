# Expansion-only on-policy prerequisite

This source prerequisite introduces one explicitly typed expansion actor and Monte Carlo
objective. It authorizes no scientific initialization, seed reservation, collection or fitting.
The existing two-head card/purchase collection, objective, optimizer and bundle contracts retain
their defaults. A later reviewed protocol must pin the concrete inputs, streams and resource limits.

## Behavior and information boundary

The controlled seat and every rival retain exact qualified G3 card/purchase policies, raw M8
ordinary behavior, the F0 purchase reference and the qualified E/T overlays. Only the controlled
seat's ordinary `expand` decisions use the new actor. Its baseline is the actual qualified E
recommendation at that freshly offered window. The expansion overlay must not replace its sampled
choice. Nested responses and all other phases retain their existing owners.

Use the complete `aor-expansion-observation-2` dictionary and authoritative canonical offer,
including Pass. Do not use the eight-arm paired-data proposals, private authority state, future
outcomes, corpus cohorts or manually priced bonuses. Record absolute decision and batch-start
numbers, original sampler seeds, full observation, selected index, log probability and value.
Every completed origin, including zero-expansion games, retains its terminal reward and identity.
Interrupted games never acquire terminal labels.

The 64-wide residual actor uses unchanged territory mathematics. Initialization transfers only
verified common RawActorState columns and bias from exact G3 Purchase; unmatched state columns
are zero. A prospective stream initializes a new action basis, with zero actor output, action
bias and value head. Shared hidden-function parity must be checked, not merely flat output parity.
Zero residual gives .97 to E and divides .03 over the other offers; a singleton gets probability
one. This is sampled behavior, not byte-identical deterministic G3 expansion play.

## Learning and publication

`expansion-terminal-mc-1` supplies detached advantage `terminalReward - 0.5` for every recorded
controlled expansion decision. Gamma and lambda are both one; the intermediate reward is zero.
The fixed state-independent baseline is valid without a critic-calibration claim. Original
behavior values, log probabilities, observations and terminal labels remain unchanged. This
prerequisite performs no value fitting, reward shaping, entropy bonus or reference imitation.

Reuse the established Float64 residual/PPO arithmetic: four epochs, Adam learning rate .0003,
clip .2 and whole-collection behavior-KL ceiling .01. An epoch restores both actor and Adam after
KL rejection or timeout. The loss remains the established uniform decision mean; independent
whole games are reporting units. Freeze/exclude both value-head parameters and momentum.

A new expansion-only checkpoint identity binds the phase, estimator, complete ecology, original
data, actor, dictionaries and recipe. Fresh Adam is followed only by the preceding completed,
exact five-slot actor Adam. Carry validates complete finite moments, nonnegative second moments,
step counts and every Adam option. It cannot consume purchase/joint checkpoints. Publish a new
actor only after its update and input checks complete; never modify G3 C/P artifacts.

## Prospective finite execution

The intended first scientific protocol is one retained four-by-256-world operational prefix,
followed by the same attempt through exactly 64 batches if predeclared integrity, exposure,
throughput and storage gates pass. These gates cannot inspect returns or policy quality. The
prefix is part of the 64 batches, not warmup or repeat data. All missing/empty cases and failed
attempts remain explicit. One fixed historical reproduction per batch uses the same collector.
No intermediate strength looks, implicit retries, shortened successful candidate, fallback or
automatic successor are permitted. Only an exact completed final actor could enter separately
reviewed fresh development and qualification; this source milestone creates neither bank.

The finite runner should reuse the existing controller, bounded child deadlines and lossless
batch/optimizer archives. Resources and the throughput gate remain unresolved until synthetic
source checks and a concrete package are reviewed; do not silently infer them from old campaigns.
