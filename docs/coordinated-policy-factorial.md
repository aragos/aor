# Frozen four-head policy diagnostic

This source-only family85 protocol describes a diagnostic, not a qualification or candidate search.
It compares every combination of the frozen83 mean16 C/P/E/T heads with the original G3 C/P and
untrained E0/T0 containers, under raw and supported policies and homogeneous and mixed rivals.
No fitting, mask nomination, promotion, activation, retry, replacement world or adaptive contrast
is permitted. Actual subjects and fresh streams require separate root preparation admission.

## Diagnostic estimands

Report all64 cell means and each cell's paired difference from mask0 in its same context.
For each of the four heads, compute the average of its eight background-specific on-minus-off
contrasts **within each world**. Report the six two-head interactions as the average of four
background-specific difference-of-differences within each world. Report Support1-minus-RAW and
MIXED-minus-HOMOGENEOUS for every mask and each head effect, always paired by world. No
outcome-dependent contrast or sorting/ranking is allowed. Support contrasts change all seats and
must not be interpreted as candidate-only effects or causal credit from the ES gradient.

Metrics are the existing authoritative controlled-seat win share (ties split), fixed reward,
rank reward and Chaos indicator. Each predeclared contrast is reduced to512 world values before
its mean and sample-standard-error calculation. Intervals use1.96 times that standard error;
they are pointwise descriptive95% intervals with no multiplicity claim, promotion gate or
bootstrap RNG. Completion requires all32 cases and exact counts before producing any effects.
