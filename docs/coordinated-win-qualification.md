# Frozen inference qualification

This versioned method qualifies one nominated complete policy against the supported G3 reference.
It does not train, nominate another candidate, activate a preview, or reinterpret the earlier
win-and-reward qualification. Source fixtures are constructed; no scientific nominee is available
until an independent completed-run audit and both development win gates admit its sole final mean.

## Portable inference and nomination

Every subject is exactly `{path, sha256}`. Asset and certificate references are normalized relative
paths confined to their owner directory. Raw bytes, complete dictionaries and reference identities
are checked before execution. No old training JAR, training scope, or lineage decoder is required.
