AoR online

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

AoR online is an online version of Age of Renaissance. This policy explains how the operator of AoR online ("we") handles information when you visit the site, create an account, or play a game.

Information we collect

  • Sign-in information. We use Google Firebase Authentication for Google sign-in and email/password accounts. Firebase processes your email address, account identifiers, sign-in provider, and email verification status. Google sign-in can also supply your basic Google profile, including your name and profile picture, to Firebase. The game server uses your Firebase account ID and email verification status; its player directory does not store your email address or Google profile. For email/password accounts, Firebase manages your password credentials. AoR online does not receive your Google password.
  • Player and game information. We store your chosen public handle, account ID, game creation permissions, game memberships, game names, actions, outcomes, and history. We also store private game information needed to resume play, such as hands, unrevealed bids, and saved or authorized action plans. Administrative records include permission changes and game management actions.
  • Technical information. Our hosting and authentication providers process information such as IP addresses, browser details, request times, and error or security logs to deliver and protect the service.
  • Messages you send us. If you contact us, we receive the contact details and information you include in your message.

How we use information

We use this information to sign you in, verify your email, identify your player account, help players find and invite each other, control access, save and replay games, execute plans you authorize, provide support, investigate errors or abuse, and keep the service secure. Firebase sends account verification and password reset emails when requested.

Google sign-in is used for authentication. We do not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or calendar. We do not sell personal information or use Google account data for advertising. Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements where applicable.

Who can see information

Your handle is visible to other registered players so they can find you and invite you to games. Other players can see game names, participants, and public game activity when browsing or spectating games. Choose a handle and game names you are comfortable sharing.

Ordinary player and spectator views hide other players' private hands, unrevealed choices, and plans until the game rules reveal them. Service administrators can access stored game records, including hidden information, for operation, support, and investigation.

We use Google Firebase for authentication and Render for application hosting and database storage. These providers process information needed to operate their services. See Firebase's privacy and security information, Google's privacy policy, and Render's privacy policy. Information may be processed in the United States and other countries where our providers operate. We may also disclose information when necessary to comply with applicable law or protect the service and its users from abuse.

Browser storage

Firebase uses browser storage to keep you signed in between visits. AoR online also saves preferences, such as game filters and setup options, in your browser. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics. You can sign out and clear this site's browser data to remove locally stored information. Clearing browser data does not delete your account or server-side game records.

Retention and security

Account and game records do not currently have an automatic expiry. We retain them to support ongoing games, replay, history, and service administration. Logs and backup copies may remain for the retention periods of our hosting and authentication providers. Ending a game or signing out does not delete its history.

We use HTTPS for the hosted service and check authentication and permissions before providing protected information. No online service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and privacy requests

You can stop using the service, sign out, and remove AoR online's access from your Google Account connections. Revoking Google access does not by itself delete your AoR online account or game history.

To ask about your information or request access, correction, or deletion, contact aor-online@googlegroups.com. Include your player handle and the email address used to sign in so we can verify your request. Never send your password or sign-in tokens. We will review requests and explain any records that need to remain for shared game history, security, backups, or legal obligations. Account deletion is handled by request; there is no self-service deletion control in the app.

Changes to this policy

We will post updates on this page and change the date above. If our handling of personal information changes materially, we will provide additional notice as appropriate.
