Age of Renaissance.
Make room at the table.

An unofficial browser version of the strategy board game. Corner the cloth market, race for advances, and hope your friends have already played their nastiest cards.

Play online →

Sign in with Google or email when you’re ready to play.

Trade, scheme, mind the misery

Take your city from its first markets through three epochs of trade and expansion. Time your commodity cards, outbid your rivals, and try to keep your people out of Chaos. The app takes care of the bookkeeping.

A long game, on your schedule

Play a few turns together or come back when it’s your move. Games are saved as you play, and the activity log helps you catch up on what everyone else has been doing. Your hand and unrevealed bids stay secret.

Get your group together

Sign in, pick a player handle, and give it to your game organizer so they can add you to the table. You can return to your games from another device, so there’s no need to leave the board set up on the dining table.
