A shared table, wherever you are

Age of Renaissance,
played together online.

AoR online brings the strategy board game to your browser. Gather 3–6 players, build a trading empire, and guide your city through three epochs.

Play online →

Sign in with Google or email when you’re ready to play.

01

Build your position

Trade in commodities, play cards, develop advances, and expand across the map. The app handles the rules and records the results of each turn.

02

Play at your own pace

Games are saved as you play. Come back for your next turn and review the activity and recorded game history to catch up with your table.

03

Keep your own hand

Each player has their own view of the game. Your cards and unrevealed bids stay hidden from other players, while spectators can follow public play.

Find your place at the table

Sign in and choose a public player handle. A game organizer can use your handle to add you to a game with friends. Your account keeps your player identity and games linked so you can return from another device.
